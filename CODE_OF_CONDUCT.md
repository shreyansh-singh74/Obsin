# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Obsin to be a welcoming project for Obsidian users, contributors, and maintainers. We pledge to make participation harassment-free regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technology preferences.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Welcome newcomers and help them get oriented.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project for users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive comments
- Publishing private information without permission
- Dismissing people based on identity or background
- Repeatedly pushing off-topic or already-declined requests

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or take other appropriate action when behavior harms the project community.

If you see a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the Contributor Covenant, adapted for a small open-source project.
